# Course 2 : Amazon Quick Foundations 

- Amazon Quick : AI-powered business intelligence and knowledge platform that helps organizations turn raw business data into insights people can easily explore, visualize, and act on. for analyzing data, automating work, building applications, and conducting research. You interact with it through natural-language chat, and it uses AI agents to work against the data and applications you connect.
- Connect data → Clean & prepare it → Analyze it → Visualize it → Query it with AI → Share insights securely
- Dashboards for visualization, Quick Index for connecting data, Quick chat for natural-language questions, and Quick spaces for collaboration.

- Quick Index : connects your documents and data so answers from Quick are grounded in your own information.
- Quick chat : is the natural-language interface for asking questions of your data.
- Quick spaces : gathers a team's dashboards, data, and documentation into one shared, permissioned hub.
- Connecting your data — Amazon Quick connects your documents and data sources so that answers are grounded in your own information rather than generic knowledge.
- Chat agents are the natural-language interface. You ask questions of your data in plain language and get answers back, instead of building a new chart for every question.
- Spaces is where teams collaborate. A space gathers dashboards, data, and documentation into one shared hub, with permissions that control who can see and change what.
